Draft terminal guide

Pick Board is the draft-day order: legal roster options first, then the uncertainty-aware wait band, then the configured same-band tiebreak, then exact wait cost. The current same-band tiebreak is consensus-first (ECR rank, then Model, then gain); it is isolated for backtesting and does not change VONA, projection weights or survival. A 25+ rank Model/ECR conflict activates a conservative review gate. Survives is the chance the player is still available at your next pick.

Wait Cost is the ceiling-adjusted value lost if you wait until your next pick — VONA, in the older label. Read the wait band first: A, B, C and so on group players whose wait costs are inside a 4–12 point uncertainty band, so small forecast differences are treated as ties instead of false precision. Everything in band A is a live option; the exact number under the letter is only the final tiebreak. The ECR T1 line under a name is the published expert tier for that player, which is a different thing from the wait band.

Projection is the median of ESPN, CBS, and FFToday when available. Each row shows the number of contributing sources, their range, and the forecast spread; that is uncertainty evidence, not a calibrated confidence grade. CBS and FFToday raw stat lines are rescored locally using player-specific 2023–25 first-down rates regressed toward position averages. ADP and ECR never enter the projection. Model subtracts 80% of position replacement from projected points, blends that core rank with half-PPR ECR re-ranked among skill players, and breaks wait-band ties. Model is an audit view, not the pick list.

ECR is one current FantasyPros expert panel, not an invitation to average the same opinions again. Its expert disagreement is shown under the ECR rank and included in Copy state for close-call judgment; it does not alter wait cost or availability. Raw ECR, ADP and ECR − Model stay off the Pick Board so the on-clock screen is short — they are one tab away in the audit views and always in Copy state. ECR − Model is the skill-player ECR rank minus the Model rank: positive means the model likes the player more than the panel does. Additional expert lists remain shadow evidence until historical testing shows incremental value.

Ceiling weight defaults to the validated 0.40 and remains live-adjustable. The current upside SD is a configured generic position-rate proxy carried separately from forecast disagreement; it is useful across positions but is not a measured player-specific ceiling. Resolve close same-position calls with current role, health, discipline and quarterback news in the copied-state judgment step.

Availability is market-first: RB/WR/TE blend 80% of Fantasy Football Calculator's current 12-team half-PPR market with 20% ESPN-only room rank/ADP, QB blends 50/50, and K/DST are unchanged since they never enter VONA. ESPN's room price for RB/WR runs later than the market; ESPN's room price for TE runs earlier than the market instead (about 15 picks among ranked TEs in the current sample). TE keeps the same 80% market weight as RB/WR for now — that is a provisional choice pending targeted validation, not a fit to this one snapshot. Observed market draft dispersion drives the survival curve when a player is matched, widened whenever ESPN and market disagree so a large source gap reads as genuine uncertainty instead of false precision; otherwise a conservative heuristic supplies the fallback. Unavailable and low-source-coverage players remain searchable for accurate bookkeeping but no longer occupy forecasted opponent picks or expected-next-player estimates.

ESPN update plus a date under a name is only the timestamp of that player's most recent ESPN news item, shown when it landed within the last three days. It is a recency marker, not a headline and not a sentiment signal — read the actual news before acting on it.

Winning build is QB1, TE1, ten RB/WR, K1 and D/ST1. It starts with three RB/WR through Round 4, secures WR1 by Round 5, fills RB2/WR2/FLEX by Round 8, TE by Round 9, QB by Round 10, and takes K/D/ST in Rounds 13–14. QB2 and TE2 are waiver positions in this shallow format.

1.03 turn planner is a read-only advisory: it separates current pick-22 targets from players likely to survive to pick 27, then offers independent pair ideas. Those pair ideas are not a joint simulation and never change the ledger or board ranking. Conditional fallers are labeled from low survival to pick 22.

Smart Queue is a model-generated, read-only advisory that appears once pick 22 is on the clock. Unlike the turn planner's pair ideas, it is a genuine conditional simulation: for each top pick-22 candidate it rolls forward opponent picks 23–26 with the same calibrated opponent model, then re-derives eligibility, roster state and the best pick-27 outcome against that specific resulting pool — not two independently ranked numbers added together. Candidates are ranked by VOR@22 + E[VOR@27], both value-over-replacement figures, which is a different quantity from the Pick Board's wait-cost/VONA gain (value vs. your next pick) — the two numbers are not directly comparable. It never changes the ledger, board ranking or your manual Queue; press Compute to run it (it does not run automatically on every render, and while it is running the button is disabled and shows Computing…).

Draft keeps the top eight candidates in the first screen and lets you scroll through the full ranking below them. On the Pick Board, press 1–8 to record one of those top eight, type an unambiguous name and press Enter, or click any player row. A partial name matching multiple players will never record automatically. Model, Projection, ECR and ADP are read-only audit views; switch back to the Pick Board before recording. A large Model/ECR gap is labeled projection-led or consensus-led for review; it does not change rank weights. Auto to my pick and Ctrl/Cmd Enter rehearse opponent picks through your next turn; simulated picks are marked ~ in the same ledger and Copy state, so reset the draft before using it live. Snake remains the planning-only board for comparing whole-room scenarios. While this guide is open the board is locked — nothing can be recorded or undone.

Queue is manual and persisted — your explicit override layer, separate from any model advisory. Use + on a Pick Board row, reorder targets under Room / Queue, and use Pick there only while you are on the clock. Tag a queued entry Target, Conditional, or Fade for your own bookkeeping; tags persist, export, and survive Reset with the entry, but never influence Model rank, wait cost, or survival. A queued player who fails the recommendation guardrails, or a Fade tagged on a current Smart Queue candidate, is flagged in the queue row so the conflict is visible before you draft it. Opponent picks move queued targets to Recently sniped, tag included; Undo reactivates them. Backup JSON copies a machine-restorable snapshot, and Import validates it before replacing local state. Reset draft preserves the queue and its tags.

Record every real pick here. Copy state on your turn and paste it to Codex or Claude for a current-news top three. K/DST stay out of wait-cost ranking and are prioritized in rounds 13–14.
