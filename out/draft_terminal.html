Draft terminal guide

Pick Board is the draft-day order: legal roster options first, then the uncertainty-aware wait band, then Model decision rank, then exact wait cost. A 25+ rank Model/ECR conflict activates a conservative review gate. Survives is the chance the player is still available at your next pick.

Wait Cost is the ceiling-adjusted value lost if you wait until your next pick — VONA, in the older label. Read the wait band first: A, B, C and so on group players whose wait costs are inside a 4–12 point uncertainty band, so small forecast differences are treated as ties instead of false precision. Everything in band A is a live option; the exact number under the letter is only the final tiebreak. The ECR T1 line under a name is the published expert tier for that player, which is a different thing from the wait band.

Points is the median of ESPN, CBS, and FFToday when available. CBS and FFToday raw stat lines are rescored locally using player-specific 2023–25 first-down rates regressed toward position averages. ADP and ECR never enter the projection. Model subtracts 80% of position replacement from projected points, blends that core rank with half-PPR ECR re-ranked among skill players, and breaks wait-band ties. Model is an audit view, not the pick list.

ECR is one current FantasyPros expert panel, not an invitation to average the same opinions again. Its expert disagreement is shown under the ECR rank and included in Copy state for close-call judgment; it does not alter wait cost or availability. Raw ECR, ADP and ECR − Model stay off the Pick Board so the on-clock screen is short — they are one tab away in the audit views and always in Copy state. ECR − Model is the skill-player ECR rank minus the Model rank: positive means the model likes the player more than the panel does. Additional expert lists remain shadow evidence until historical testing shows incremental value.

Ceiling weight defaults to the validated 0.40 and remains live-adjustable. The current upside SD is a configured generic position-rate proxy carried separately from forecast disagreement; it is useful across positions but is not a measured player-specific ceiling. Resolve close same-position calls with current role, health, discipline and quarterback news in the copied-state judgment step.

Availability is market-first: RB/WR/TE blend 80% of Fantasy Football Calculator's current 12-team half-PPR market with 20% ESPN-only room rank/ADP, QB blends 50/50, and K/DST are unchanged since they never enter VONA. ESPN's room price for RB/WR/TE runs consistently later than the market, so it is deliberately shrunk toward market rather than trusted outright. Observed market draft dispersion drives the survival curve when a player is matched, widened whenever ESPN and market disagree so a large source gap reads as genuine uncertainty instead of false precision; otherwise a conservative heuristic supplies the fallback. Unavailable and low-confidence players remain searchable for accurate bookkeeping but no longer occupy forecasted opponent picks or expected-next-player estimates.

ESPN update plus a date under a name is only the timestamp of that player's most recent ESPN news item, shown when it landed within the last three days. It is a recency marker, not a headline and not a sentiment signal — read the actual news before acting on it.

Winning build is QB1, TE1, ten RB/WR, K1 and D/ST1. It starts with three RB/WR through Round 4, secures WR1 by Round 5, fills RB2/WR2/FLEX by Round 8, TE by Round 9, QB by Round 10, and takes K/D/ST in Rounds 13–14. QB2 and TE2 are waiver positions in this shallow format.

Draft keeps the top eight candidates in the first screen and lets you scroll through the full ranking below them. On the Pick Board, press 1–8 to record one of those top eight, type a name and press Enter, or click any player row. Model, Points, ECR and ADP are read-only audit views; switch back to the Pick Board before recording. Auto to my pick and Ctrl/Cmd Enter rehearse opponent picks through your next turn; simulated picks are marked ~ in the same ledger and Copy state, so reset the draft before using it live. Snake remains the planning-only board for comparing whole-room scenarios. While this guide is open the board is locked — nothing can be recorded or undone.

Record every real pick here. Copy state on your turn and paste it to Codex or Claude for a current-news top three. K/DST stay out of wait-cost ranking and are prioritized in rounds 13–14.
