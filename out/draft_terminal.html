Draft terminal guide

VONA is the ceiling-adjusted value lost if you wait until your next pick. Players within 7.5 VONA points share a tier, then Model rank breaks the near-tie. Survives is the chance the player is still available then.

Points is the median of ESPN, CBS, and FFToday when available. CBS and FFToday raw stat lines are rescored locally for this league; ADP and ECR never enter the projection. The source range appears under each recommendation. Model adds replacement value, the existing ceiling/upside weighting, and a 20% half-PPR ECR sanity check. Projection uncertainty is tracked separately so disagreement is not mistaken for upside. Δ is Model rank versus ECR: a positive number means the model is higher on the player.

Availability comes from ESPN. Injured players stay searchable so every real pick can be recorded, while zero-projection and long-term unavailable players are excluded from automatic recommendations. Bye weeks come from the official NFL schedule and are informational—not a reason to pass on an elite player.

Winning build defaults to one QB, one TE, and ten RB/WR. It starts with three RB/WR through Round 4, completes every skill starter by Round 8, and takes K and D/ST in Rounds 13–14. One late QB2 or TE2 may replace a depth pick only when it beats the best legal RB/WR by a full value threshold; TE has the lower hurdle because it can fill FLEX. Players that would block the path stay searchable but rank below eligible recommendations.

Draft shows the full available pool and lets you sort by VONA, Model, Points, ECR, or ADP. Auto to my pick records varied need-aware opponent selections until your next turn; use the footer button or press ⌘ Enter on Mac / Ctrl Enter on Windows. ~ marks those picks in the log and copied state. Snake is a separate full-width board. Model and ECR follow their source; Need-aware follows a 65% ESPN ADP, 25% ESPN draft-room rank, 10% ECR timing blend while adjusting for each team's roster. Its randomness scales with observed ADP dispersion, calibrated on 2018–2024 draft markets. D/ST is an early-season streaming board weighted 55% Week 1, 25% Week 2, 10% Week 3, 7.5% season projection, and 2.5% ECR. Kicker uses 40% Week 1, 20% Week 2, 10% Week 3, 22.5% season projection, and 7.5% ECR so strong holds stay high while the fallback list remains matchup-aware. Snake never records picks.

Record every real pick here. Copy state on your turn and paste it to Codex or Claude for a reasoned top three. K/DST stay out of VONA, are prioritized in rounds 13–14, and remain visible/selectable throughout the draft.
