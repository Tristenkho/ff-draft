Draft terminal guide

Gain is the ceiling-adjusted value lost if you wait until your next pick. Survives is the chance the player is still available then.

Model uses custom league scoring, ceiling weighting, value over replacement, and a 20% half-PPR ECR sanity check. Δ is Model rank versus ECR: a positive number means the model is higher on the player.

Draft shows the full available pool and lets you sort by VONA, Model, Points, ECR, or ADP. Auto to my pick records varied need-aware opponent selections until your next turn; ~ marks those picks in the log and copied state. Snake is a separate full-width board. Model, ECR, and ADP follow their source exactly; Need-aware simulates each team filling starters, FLEX, depth, and late special teams. Randomize adds optional variation. Snake never records picks.

Record every real pick here. Copy state on your turn and paste it to Codex or Claude for a reasoned top three. K/DST stay out of VONA, are prioritized in rounds 13–14, and remain visible/selectable throughout the draft.
