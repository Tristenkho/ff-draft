# One private league app across seasons

The product combines draft preparation/history and season research in one private application for the user's league, with separate modules sharing player identity, scoring and evidence. ESPN is authoritative for current rosters and submitted lineups; completed draft picks and captured draft-time evidence are reconciled and frozen by season, with versioned corrections. This replaces the proposed separate season product while preventing live transactions from rewriting draft history; the standalone terminal remains a migration fallback.
